# Company knowledge audit checklist

Use before updating FAQ knowledge from company materials.

## Validate against official source
- Company overview and positioning
- Product/service descriptions
- Office count and office locations
- Contact channels
- Pricing posture
- Industry fit / target customers

## Flag separately before pushing
- Date-bound operational notices
- Certifications with exact standard/version numbers
- Exclusive market-position claims (for example, "only", "first", "sole")
- Performance percentages / delivery-rate metrics
- Named-client claims when source only shows logos or testimonials

## Recommended mutation pattern
1. List existing FAQ.
2. Compare each overlapping FAQ to official sources.
3. Update stale or mismatched records first.
4. Add missing high-frequency customer questions second.
5. Verify final FAQ inventory after push.
6. Record payload paths and outcomes in changelog.
